**Macroscopic Emission Modeling of Urban Traffic Using Probe Vehicle Data**

First-author paper published at **IEEE Big Data 2024** (Washington, D.C.), conducted at **Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory**.

### Highlights

- Processed and analyzed a **272M-row spatio-temporal dataset** of traffic density, network characteristics, vehicle types, and emissions across U.S. urban census tracts (New York, Colorado, Texas, Georgia)
- Leveraged **multiprocessing on 60 CPU cores** to accelerate data cleaning and preprocessing
- Built **end-to-end predictive ML pipelines** (LightGBM, CatBoost, XGBoost, neural networks)
- Integrated **ETL data engineering**, automated preprocessing, feature engineering, SHAP-based explainability
- Full **MLOps workflow** for reproducible large-scale training and validation
- Awarded a **$10,000 Excellence Fellowship**

### Tech Stack

Python, LightGBM, CatBoost, XGBoost, SHAP, Pandas, multiprocessing, MLflow
